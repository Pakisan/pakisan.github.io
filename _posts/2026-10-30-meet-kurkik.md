# Meet Kurkik

🎃 Something stirred in the stables of the AsyncAPI JetBrains plugin…

A pale, strange, slightly spooky horse has escaped, just before Halloween. 🐴🌕

Meet **Kurkik**, named after Կուռկիկ Ջալալի, the legendary horse from the Armenian epic.

For a few releases, Kurkik lived quietly inside the [plugin](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/plugin/15673-asyncapi). It powered everything that touches your messaging systems: Apache Kafka, Apache Pulsar, Amazon SNS and Amazon SQS. It helped you inspect, export and diff your connections between environments, or against your source code.

## Beyond the IDE

But a horse like this can't stay in the IDE forever. 🕯️
Now Kurkik has its own body and is ready to serve your whole team, outside of IDEs:

- 🦇 **Guards against API drift** between your AsyncAPI documents and the brokers that actually run
- 🕸️ **Keeps APIs self-maintaining.** Export, compare and fix, all from one AsyncAPI document
- 🪦 **Makes setups repeatable.** Apply one document to dev, staging and prod, with a plan you approve

## Vendor-neutral by design

Kurkik plays well with others. It is vendor-neutral, tied to no broker vendor or platform. It speaks one recognisable format, AsyncAPI, so the documents it produces can also configure API gateways or feed docs in EventCatalog.
